# A visit from Apollo 12

**Level:** Level 2 (after power is restored)  
**Title:** A visit from Apollo 12  

## Text
In November 1969, Apollo 12 astronauts removed parts from Surveyor 3, including its TV camera, and returned them to Earth. The rest of the lander remains on the Moon.
